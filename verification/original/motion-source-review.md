# Quiet Form source and runtime motion review

Reviewed November 16, 2026. `src/variants/minimal.tsx`, `minimal-supplement.tsx` and `minimal.css` have no motion imports, authored keyframes, transitions, timers for decorative motion, animated SVG/canvas or View Transition calls. The shared Button and Radix Dialog composition omit shadcn animation utilities. No `tw-animate-css` dependency/import is installed. The shared viewer, switcher, form and media controls are static. CSS transforms on the skip link and centered modal are instantaneous positioning states, without interpolated motion. The form's 650ms timer simulates pending work with static text and is invalidated by reset/unmount; it is not a motion effect.

The scoped `.minimal` guard covers both the experience and the explicit minimal portal scope. Its reset is additional defense: the reachable components themselves are authored without animation. Interactive/vision transitions are scoped to their own class; no global motion styles are imported. Local gallery assets are still JPEG/PNG. The single H.264 clip has no autoplay or loop and plays only on explicit user input; its content motion is allowed by the specification. Cross-origin school recordings are static external references, not animated embeds.

`motion-results.json`: 20 runtime states, Chromium 159.0.8154.6, normal `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`, observation installed before load. No animation/transition start events, active WAAPI animations, nonzero computed transition durations, animation names, smooth scroll containers, autoplay or looping media were found. All elements and ::before/::after pseudo-elements were inspected, including the portal. Cold minimal entry and both prior-variant switch paths were exercised. Hover/focus, lightbox, disclosure, menu, filters, route change and form invalid/pending/success/failure states are included. No suppression styles were injected and screenshots use animations: allow. The reviewed state captures show complete instantaneous layouts; settled-animation polling alone is not the evidence.

Native video controls, normal scrolling, caret movement and explicit video playback are browser/content behavior. This does not claim access to cross-origin player internals or every possible browser implementation.
